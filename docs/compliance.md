# Customer information evidence

Checked 11 October 2026. These checks support an operator's privacy review. They do not certify compliance, determine lawful collection, or replace a privacy officer's assessment.

| Rule | Record check | Source |
|---|---|---|
| NZ information privacy principle 3 | Flag an account without a notice assessment and supporting evidence | [OPC: direct collection](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3/) |
| NZ information privacy principle 3A | Use the same assessment field to record indirect-collection notice obligations or an applicable exception, including imported contact details | [OPC: indirect collection](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3a/) |
| NZ information privacy principle 5 | Flag a missing or past security review date | [OPC: safeguards](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) |
| NZ information privacy principle 9 | Flag a missing or past retention review date | [OPC: retention](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) |

The dates are local review reminders chosen by the business. No statute sets these review intervals. A future review date and a note do not prove the underlying safeguards exist. The system never deletes records automatically. Review customer contacts, usage evidence, drafts, exports and backups together. Avoid passwords, payment details and sensitive personal information in notes.

This base ships NZ evidence reminders. Australian and other obligations need a jurisdiction-specific assessment before deployment. No marketing consent or permission to send is inferred from a contact record.

RLS is enabled with no public policies. The CLI uses a trusted operator database connection. It does not authenticate individual employees. Activity records identify the supplied actor but are not tamper-proof. Configure restricted database roles, backups, a restore rehearsal and access controls before shared operation.
