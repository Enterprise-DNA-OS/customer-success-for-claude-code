## Product and import facts

- [Totango identity announcement](https://www.totango.com/): now Odie. The former /pricing address returned 404 this run. No assertion about present plan prices or tiers is supported.
- [Segment exports](https://support.totango.com/hc/en-us/articles/10233417253652-Export-segment-data): CSV headings follow selected segment columns.
- [Segments](https://support.totango.com/hc/en-us/articles/208774826-Understand-segments): accounts, users, tasks, touchpoints and other objects can be segmented.
- [Health](https://support.totango.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032136152-Analyze-health-at-the-account-level): configurable profiles, history and unknown health exist. We do not claim those capabilities are absent.
- [Task and touchpoint exports](https://support.totango.com/hc/en-us/articles/4404703957524-Export-a-local-data-file): separate files, not imported by the account loader.

The local schema and health thresholds are our design. They are not a reconstruction of proprietary Totango internals. The demo output is generated from fictional data.
